About

Technova 2K26

TECHNOVA 2K26 is the national level inter college symposium of the ISTE Student Chapter at CSI College of Engineering, Ketti. For two days in the Nilgiris, students from across the country meet to design, build, argue and perform.

Eight events across two tracks. One is engineering under pressure — drafting, circuits, pitching, problem solving. The other belongs to voice, memory and imagination. Both reward the same thing: doing the work well, in front of people.

The host

A campus in the clouds

CSI College of Engineering stands at Ketti, on the road that climbs from Coonoor to Ooty. Founded by the CSI Coimbatore Diocesan Council, teaching since 1998, and now autonomous by UGC conferment — nine departments, one hill campus, and the ground TECHNOVA 2K26 is built on.
